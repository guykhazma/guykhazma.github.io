Hi! I am a Research Staff Member at [IBM Research](https://www.research.ibm.com/labs/haifa/) where I work on big data analytics as part of the [Cloud and Data Technologies Group](https://www.research.ibm.com/haifa/dept/stt/ssp.shtml).
I am also a co-creator of the open source project [Xskipper](https://xskipper.io/master/) - an Extensible Data Skipping Framework for Apache Spark.   
In addition, I am currently studying toward an MBA degree at Tel Aviv University.


Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from Tel Aviv University.

My research interests are in distributed systems, large-scale data processing and infrastructure for data analytics.
