I am a PhD student at the University of Toronto, where I am advised by Professor [Eyal de Lara](http://www.cs.toronto.edu/~delara/) and Professor [Niv Dayan](https://www.nivdayan.net/). My work focuses on key-value stores, storage engines and distributed systems, including key-value separation in LSM-trees ([Wayfinder](https://doi.org/10.1145/3663351.3663880)) and linearizable local reads in distributed systems ([Asymmetric Linearizable Local Reads](https://doi.org/10.14778/3742728.3742738)).

Before starting my PhD, I was a Cloud Data Researcher at [IBM Research](https://research.ibm.com/labs/israel), where I worked on big data analytics as part of the Cloud and Data Technologies group. There I co-created the open source project [Xskipper](https://xskipper.io/master/), an extensible data skipping framework for Apache Spark, and worked on JSON CRDTs for document stores ([DSON](https://doi.org/10.14778/3510397.3510403)). Between 2024 and 2026, I also worked as a Senior Software Engineer at IBM, leading efforts around the Apache Iceberg and Delta Lake open table formats.

Before joining IBM, I received my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from Tel Aviv University and an MBA degree from Tel Aviv University.

My research interests are in key-value stores, distributed systems, databases, and infrastructure for large-scale data processing.
